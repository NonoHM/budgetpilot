# AISVS 1.0 map of the AI path

For a security reviewer checking a claim about the AI path, and a contributor closing one of its
gaps. What the connection still holds in 1.3 is in [the local model connection](./ai-model.md).

## Status in 1.3: no AI use

Release 1.3 makes no model call. The dashboard advice this map used to assess was removed
([#1010](https://github.com/NonoHM/budgetpilot/issues/1010)) under the decision record
[#1009](https://github.com/NonoHM/budgetpilot/issues/1009), and the connection to Ollama is kept as
a part with no caller. **The product rows below describe 1.3.** A requirement that attaches to a
model call is `n/a`; one that attaches to a part 1.3 ships keeps a verdict read from that part.
The resulting drop in `partly` and `unmet` closes no gap: the section « Counts » says what it means.

The verdicts the removed advice earned, with their evidence, are in this file's history at the
commit before #1010 (`a4f3f2c` on `main`). The next feature that calls a model re-resolves every row
it touches in its own pull request (B4, [#1016](https://github.com/NonoHM/budgetpilot/issues/1016)).
The development rows are about how the repository is built and are unaffected.

Every Level 1 and Level 2 requirement of OWASP AISVS 1.0, with its verdict against what
BudgetPilot 1.3 ships of the AI path, the evidence, and the issue that closes each gap. It is the AI
path's counterpart of the ASVS 5.0.0 position in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

**A snapshot of one branch.** Assessed on 2026-10-11 against the branch of #1010. A change that
moves a row edits that row in the same pull request; nothing else re-derives a verdict.

## What was assessed

**The AI path of 1.3, defined by what ships:** the connection in
`src/lib/server/insights/local-llm.ts` (host allowlist, redirect guard, reachability probe and the
transport `requestLocalModel`), which nothing calls; `src/lib/server/ai/admission.ts`, a library
with no caller; the `LLM_*` and `BP_LLM_*` settings, checked at start and read by no feature; the
ESLint rule that refuses any import of the transport under `src/` (`modelDoor.spec.ts`);
`scripts/ensure-ollama.mjs` (`npm run setup:llm`, `npm run dev:ai`); the model pin
`src/lib/server/ai/qualification/qualified-model.json`; and the Ollama service in
`docker-compose.ai.yml` and `docker-compose.ai.gpu.yml`. The rule-based insights call no model and
are not assessed.

**How a row is decided.** A requirement about a model call in operation (what reaches a model, what
its answer becomes, a member's use of AI output, the logging of inference) is `n/a`, because 1.3
makes no model call. A requirement about a part 1.3 ships (the transport's own checks, the overlay,
the pull and the pin) keeps a verdict read from that part.

**The configuration:** `LLM_ENABLED=true` with the Compose overlay the repository ships, which starts
Ollama and turns the connection on while nothing asks it anything. With Ollama on the host or at a
remote address, the deployment rows below describe what the operator has to provide.

**Level 3 requirements are not mapped.**

## Verdicts

Six verdicts, ruled by the owner on 2026-10-09 (#841):

| Verdict   | Meaning                                                                                                                                |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| met       | The code or configuration does what the requirement asks. The evidence names where.                                                    |
| partly    | Part of it holds. The evidence says what holds and what is missing, and the issue closes the rest.                                     |
| unmet     | Nothing does it. The issue closes it.                                                                                                  |
| n/a       | The requirement has nothing to attach to in this product. The reason is a fact about the product.                                      |
| operator  | Outside the repository's control: the model's vendor or the operator's host. The evidence says what the repository provides toward it. |
| exception | The owner ruled that the requirement will not be met. The evidence gives the reason and the compensating control.                      |

« Exception candidate » beside an issue means an exception is proposed and not yet ruled: the row
counts under its verdict until the owner rules.

**Basis**, the strongest one that applies: `attack` (observed on a build by an attack run; the
hostile-model run below was on the removed advice, and no current row rests on it), `test` (a spec
pins it, named), `CI` or `hook` (a gate enforces it), `vendor documentation`, or `reading` (the code
was read and nothing executed it).

The AI chain of release 1.3 (R16 on #841) was to close every `partly` and `unmet` product row, a
closing figure of `partly` plus `unmet` equal to 0 in the product total below. #1009 re-scoped it:
the advice left, so the rows that attached to it are `n/a`. The `partly` and `unmet` rows left
attach to parts 1.3 ships. A2 (#821), A3 (#967) and A4 (#986) are the 1.3 pull requests filed
against some of them, and the figure passes to B4 (#1016) with the rest.

### EU AI Act scope

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, read 2026-10-09 in the Official Journal
(http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj).

- **1.3 puts no AI feature into service.** It makes no model call (#1009): the dashboard advice was
  removed in #1010, and the connection to Ollama it keeps has no caller. No member reads, receives
  or interacts with anything a model produced.
- **Not high-risk.** Annex III point 5(b) lists « AI systems intended to be used to evaluate the
  creditworthiness of natural persons or establish their credit score ». 1.3 evaluates nobody with
  a model; the removed advice was for the person whose data it read and decided nothing about them.
- **A person using it at home is not a deployer.** Article 2(10): the Regulation « does not apply
  to obligations of deployers who are natural persons using AI systems in the course of a purely
  personal non-professional activity ».
- **Article 50 has nothing to attach to in 1.3.** Article 2(12) exempts systems « released under
  free and open-source licences, unless they are placed on the market or put into service as
  high-risk AI systems or as an AI system that falls under Article 5 or 50 ». Article 50(1) asks
  that people « are informed that they are interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious »,
  and Article 50(2) that generated text be « marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as
  artificially generated ». 1.3 shows no generated text and offers nothing to interact with. The
  removed advice carried a visible AI label and `data-ai-generated="true"` (G3, #969). Article 50
  applies from 2 August 2026 (Article 113).

This paragraph is the scope statement AGENTS.md asks for, and it changes when the feature does: the
next caller of the model (#1016) reopens it, and so does a remote provider, a decision about the
user, or a use beyond the user's own budget.
